what did it mean by split by document?

[AI]
"Split by document" means: when you divide your corpus into train/validation/test, you
decide the split at the level of a whole work (a whole play, a whole poem, the whole
Sonnets section) — not at the level of individual pages.

So in practice for your corpus: you'd pick, say, Othello entirely for the test set —
every single page of it, start to finish — rather than putting page 1171 in test and
page 1172 in train.

Why this matters (the leakage risk): if you split page-by-page instead, you could end up
with, say, page 1171 of Othello in your training set and page 1172 of the same play in
your test set. Since those two pages are about the same characters, same plot, same
vocabulary, same writing style — your system would basically be tested on something it
already partly saw. Your test score would look better than it really is, because the
"unseen" test page isn't actually all that unseen — it's sitting right next to material
the system trained on.

Splitting by whole document avoids that: if Othello is entirely in the test set, the
system genuinely never saw any of it during training — the test result actually
reflects how well it generalizes to new material, not how well it memorized something
adjacent to what it already had.

That's also exactly why the Sonnets question we logged earlier matters — holding out "a
handful of sonnets" from inside one document, instead of the whole document, is a small
version of this same leak.
